About Us

Soo Tamil Association

  • About Us
  • Mission
  • Vision
What began in 2018 as a small group of Tamil families in Sault Ste. Marie has blossomed into a registered not-for-profit organization, officially incorporated under the Societies Act of Ontario in 2024. Our founding members hail from diverse corners of the world — India, Sri Lanka, Malaysia, Singapore, and beyond — united by a shared love for Tamil heritage and the desire to build a strong, supportive community in Canada.
The Soo Tamil Association is dedicated to preserving and promoting the Tamil language, arts, and traditions through inclusive programs, cultural celebrations, and educational initiatives. We strive to support newcomers, foster intergenerational bonds, encourage community service, and provide spaces for artistic expression — all while creating a welcoming environment where Tamil identity flourishes within a diverse Canadian society.
To build a vibrant, united Tamil community in Sault Ste. Marie that celebrates its rich heritage, empowers future generations and thrives as a beacon of cultural pride, inclusivity, and lifelong connection.
